HOW THIS STRATEGY WORKS

Core idea: price only becomes a real support/resistance point ("solidified") after it gets swept and then structure breaks the other way to confirm the sweep was a trap, not real supply/demand. A raw swing high or low means nothing on its own — it has to be tested and rejected.

Step 1 — HTF Solidified Breakout: find a Solidified High/Low on the 1H chart, then wait for price to break back through it. That break is what defines the new range and which direction (long/short) is even on the table.

Step 2 — Last Opposite Push: the last candle/wick against the breakout direction right before it happened — this is the order block, the zone institutional flow likely originated from.

Step 3 — Target Solidified: after the breakout runs, it needs to make a new high/low and then get rejected (a close back through the swing point right before that high/low) before it counts as a real target. Until that rejection happens, it's just "the current high" — not a confirmed level yet, and this dashboard will say so explicitly rather than assume it.

Step 4 — Retrace & Shift: price needs to pull back into the Step 2 zone. Simply touching the zone isn't enough — this dashboard flags it as "watching" once price is back in range, but the actual structure shift confirmation happens on the 1-minute chart (Step 5), not automatically here.

Step 5 — 1m Micro Displacement: inside the Step 2 zone, on the 1-minute chart, price needs to break its own recent 1m swing point with real displacement — this is what actually flips the zone from "watching" to "tradeable." This dashboard gates this step to the 30-minute pre-NY-open window and does not fabricate a 1m signal outside it.

Step 6 — Entry & Risk: once Step 5 fires, the entry is the retest of the 1m order block found in Step 5. Stop-loss goes just beyond the 1m Solidified Low/High from that same step. Target is the Step 3 level.
